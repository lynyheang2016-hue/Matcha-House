About Us🍵🍃

We didn’t start this to follow trends — we started because we genuinely love matcha. The kind you enjoy in the morning or reach for during a quiet pause in the day. Based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, we opened our shop last year with a simple goal: to share good matcha we truly believe in. Thoughtfully made, easy to enjoy, and meant to be savored.
If matcha is already part of your life, welcome home. If you’re new, come try a cup with us 🌿
